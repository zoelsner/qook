Privacy Policy

Effective March 2026

Qook is a meal planning app that uses your preferences and voice input to suggest personalized recipes. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we share with third parties.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — your email address and name, provided at sign-up.
  • Meal preferences — cuisines you like or love, proteins, and cooking tools you've told us about.
  • Dietary restrictions — ingredients and allergens you want to avoid.
  • Household size — the number of people you cook for, used to scale recipe serving sizes.
  • Voice recordings — if you use the voice input feature, your audio is recorded and transcribed. Recordings are processed in real time and are not stored by Qook after transcription.

How We Use Your Data

Your preferences, dietary restrictions, and household size are used to generate recipe suggestions tailored to you. Voice input is transcribed and used as an additional context signal for recipe generation. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party AI Services

Qook sends data to the following third-party services to generate recipes and transcribe voice input:

  • OpenRouter / Anthropic Claude — your meal preferences, dietary restrictions, and any voice transcript are sent to Claude models to generate recipe text and images. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: anthropic.com/privacy
  • OpenAI Whisper — voice recordings are sent to OpenAI for transcription. OpenAI's privacy policy applies: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

We share only the minimum data needed to fulfill each request. We do not sell your data to any third party, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Data Storage

Your account data and preferences are stored in a secure cloud database (Convex). Preferences are retained until you delete your account. Voice recordings are not stored after transcription.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Upon deletion, your preferences and account information will be removed from our systems.

Children

Qook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make material changes, we will notify you within the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@qook.app
