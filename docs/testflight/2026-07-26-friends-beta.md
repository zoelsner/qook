# Qook friends beta

## TestFlight setup

- Testing type: External Testing
- Group name: Friends beta
- Distribution: Public link
- Tester limit: 15
- Build: 0.1.0 (5)

Friends do not need App Store Connect access and their email addresses are not
required. They install Apple's free TestFlight app, open the public invitation
link, and accept the beta.

## Beta app description

Qook helps you decide what's for dinner based on the energy you actually have.
Choose your bandwidth, review a small hand of dinner ideas, plan the week, and
turn your picks into a grocery list.

## What to test

Use Qook to plan at least three dinners, take the Shop list on a real grocery
run, and cook one recipe. Please send TestFlight feedback or a screenshot
whenever anything feels confusing, slow, or different from what you expected.

## Message to friends

Hey — I'm testing Qook, a dinner-planning app I've been building. Would you use
it for a few dinners this week? Try planning at least three nights, use the
grocery list, and cook one recipe. Please screenshot and text me the moment
anything feels confusing. Here's the TestFlight link: [PASTE LINK]

## App Store Connect steps

1. Open Qook in App Store Connect and select TestFlight.
2. Wait for build 5 to finish processing.
3. Under External Testing, create the group `Friends beta`.
4. Add build 5 and use the beta description and What to Test copy above.
5. Submit the first external build for TestFlight App Review.
6. After approval, open the group's Testers tab and create a public link.
7. Set the link to Open to Anyone with a limit of 15, then paste it into the
   friend message above.

## Before inviting

- Confirm the TestFlight feedback email is an inbox you actively read.
- Install the build on Zach's physical iPhone first.
- Verify Apple sign-in, one live generation, recipe detail, Plan, Shop, and
  account deletion.
- Keep the first cohort small; expand only after the first few complete sessions.
